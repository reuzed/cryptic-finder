## Wordplay Types

Standard cryptic stuff:

- Concatenation
- Insertion/Deletion
- Anagram
- Alternate letters
- Reversal
- Palindrome
- Hidden

Other:

- Braided: multiple words making an alternate letters type thing, eg. applied -> ald, pi, pe ?
- Contains a given word or substring, enter say "ment" and get parliament...
- Multi word anagram: split a phrase (set of chars) into multiple words, optimising for fewest words, most similar length maybe
- Two substrings make a word, generalised braiding
- Caesar cipher pairs of words, do this for any caesar amount
- No "a"s, restricted alphabet etc.
